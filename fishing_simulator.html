⚖️ CoreSuite Balanced MMO Fishing Economics

This simulator uses the exact production mathematics implemented in CustomFishingModule.java.

1. Why Common Fish Never Disappear (The 20M Coin Exploit Guardrail)

In older uncalibrated systems, high rod upgrades and baits could crush Common drops to ~1% and inflate high-tier catches to over 94%. If an end-game player with 20,000,000 coins bought a Netherite rod and camped the Legendary Pond with Mythic Chum, they would catch endless Legendary and Epic fish in streaks, minting hundreds of millions of coins and causing hyperinflation that breaks the entire server economy.

Following top MMO standards (Hypixel SkyBlock Trophy Fishing & Sea Creature Chance, OSRS, and PyroFishing):
• Common fish still drop ~1 in 4 times (24.9%) even in the Legendary Pond with maxed Netherite gear.
• Total low-tier fish (Common + Uncommon) stay healthy at ~48% to 52%.
• Legendary catches drop at ~5.7% (1 in 17 casts), making them thrilling, rewarding milestones rather than spam drops.

2. How Gear Scaling with Diminishing Returns Works

Instead of exponential compounding multipliers, CoreSuite applies bounded square-root diminishing returns: 

gearBoost = (luck * 0.06) + (lureLvl * 0.07 * tierMult) + (sqrt(streak) * 0.03) + (baitBonus * 0.30)
mythicalWeight *= (1.0 + gearBoost * 1.2)
legendaryWeight *= (1.0 + gearBoost * 1.0)
epicWeight *= (1.0 + gearBoost * 0.8)
rareWeight *= (1.0 + gearBoost * 0.5)
commonWeight /= (1.0 + gearBoost * 0.45)
uncommonWeight /= (1.0 + gearBoost * 0.15)

3. How Fish Value & Weight Are Calculated

• Weight Multiplier: (1.0 + heavyLvl * 0.05 * tierMult + baitWeightBonus) * eventWeightMult
• Legendary Pond: Grants an instant 2.0x weight multiplier, turning 90kg catches into 180kg beasts!
• Value: Value = Weight (kg) * PricePerKg (uncapped by default, so lore prices always match payouts 100%).
