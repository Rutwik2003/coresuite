Who is the Ancient Angler?

The Ancient Angler is a mythical entity dwelling in the abyssal trenches of Minecraft. He does not spawn naturally in towns or ports—he must be tracked down and summoned by mortals who possess the fabled Tidecaller's Pearl.

Unlike standard quest NPCs who hand out simple chores, the Ancient Angler tests your nautical philosophy, patience, and resolve. He speaks in cryptic sea riddles and observes whether you choose the path of Endurance, Greed, Predation, Silence, or Storm.

💡
Dynamic Encounters: The Angler tracks your history! If you disrespect him, he will shout "YOU DARE RETURN?!" upon resummoning. As you complete his trials multiple times, his dialogue softens to acknowledge your relentless dedication to the depths.
🧭

Phase 1: The Tidecaller's Pearl & The Ritual

Before you can speak with the Angler, you must locate his hidden coastline. Here is the exact technical flow:

1

Fish Up the Pearl

The Tidecaller's Pearl (Heart of the Sea) has a base catch chance of 0.01% (1 in 10,000).
• Treasure Hunter Enchant: Adds +0.01% per level (up to 0.04%).
• Pity Guarantee: If you reach 2,500 catches without finding one, the pearl is 100% guaranteed on your next catch!

2

Follow the Helix Trail

Right-clicking the Pearl releases a dual-particle beam (End Rod & Soul Fire Flame) that spirals towards his lodestone coordinate.
• Action bar shows: "The pearl hums softly... you are X blocks away."
• Must be in the same world as the lodestone.

3

The Deep-Sea Summoning

When within 15 blocks of the target location, right-click the Pearl to initiate the ritual:
• 40 ticks of Nautilus particles and Warden heartbeat.
• Giant 200-particle water eruption + Elder Guardian sound.
• Server-wide broadcast announcement!
• Converts Pearl $ o$ permanent Angler's Tracker.

🌊
Water Summoning Mechanics: If you summon the Angler in open water, CoreSuite automatically places a magical Lily Pad underneath his feet and prevents water displacement so he never drowns or glitches into the seabed!

Offerings & The Sacrifice Hint System

Whenever you interact with the Angler during an active quest stage, you are given 3 choices:

1

[✦ Offer the Catch]

Submits the required fish from your inventory or fish bag. If correct, advances to the next stage or claims your relic!

2

[Listen to Riddle]

The Angler repeats his poetic riddle for free: "The deep speaks in riddles, kid. Only those who listen survive."

3

[✗ Sacrifice for Hint]

Pay $25,000 + 1x Rare/Epic/Legendary fish tribute to force the Angler to speak plain English (e.g. "A Char. Bring me a Char.").

⚠️
What if you choose Hint with NO rare fish tribute?
If you attempt to buy a hint without a qualifying fish in your inventory or fish bag, the Angler punishes you with a roulette penalty:
• Roll 1: Slowness I curse for 3 minutes.
• Roll 2: Mining Fatigue I curse for 3 minutes.
• Roll 3: Seizes $50,000 instead of $25,000 with thunder and lightning!
⚠️

The Consequences of Deceit & Failure

The Ancient Angler does not tolerate mortals who attempt to trick him with empty hands or wrong fish. Every time you click [Offer Catch] without the required items, your persistent Fail Counter increases:

Fail Streak Money Fine Deducted Debuff Effects Applied Angler Action
1st Fail $10,000 Blindness (8 seconds) Lightning strike sound + angry rebuke
2nd Fail $20,000 Blindness (11 seconds) Rebukes your deceit
3rd Fail $40,000 Blindness (14s) + Slowness (25s) Disorients player movement
4th Fail $80,000 Blindness (17s) + Slowness (25s) Furious storm winds
5th+ Fails $160,000 – $500,000 (Cap) Blindness (20-30s) + Slowness + Nausea (15s) Maximum abyssal curse

* Fines allow negative debt if enabled in config. Always double-check your inventory and fish bag before clicking [Offer Catch]!
