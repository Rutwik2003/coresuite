Who is the Ancient Angler?

The primordial master of the abyssal trenches.

The Ancient Angler is a mythical entity dwelling in the deep ocean depths of Minecraft. Unlike ordinary quest NPCs who stand statically at spawn or ports, the Ancient Angler must be tracked down and summoned using the mythical Tidecaller's Pearl.

He does not hand out mundane fetch quests. Instead, he tests each mortal's nautical philosophy, patience, and resolve. Depending on how you answer his philosophical questions, he will test you across Endurance, Greed, Predation, Silence, Submergence, or Storm Mastery. Beware: insolent or arrogant replies will provoke his abyssal wrath!

The Ancient Angler maintains a persistent database record for every player. When summoned, his opening dialogue dynamically reacts to your previous actions:

Phase 1: The Tidecaller's Pearl & Summoning Ritual

Acquiring the pearl, following the helix beacon, and the summoning event.

Before you can speak with the Ancient Angler, you must locate his hidden coastline:

1

Catching the Pearl

The Tidecaller's Pearl (Heart of the Sea) is fished from any open water body.
• Base Chance: 0.01% (1 in 10,000 catches).
• Treasure Hunter Enchant: Adds +0.01% per level (up to 0.04%).
• Pity Guarantee: If a player reaches 2,500 catches without finding one, the pearl drops with 100% certainty on the 2,501st catch!

2

The Helix Particle Trail

Right-clicking the Pearl releases a dual-particle beam (End Rod & Soul Fire Flame) that spirals towards his lodestone coordinate (up to 5,000 blocks away).
• Action Bar HUD: Displays "Distance to Ancient Angler: Xm" every second.
• Trail Cooldown: 10-second cooldown between beam pulses.

3

The Deep-Sea Summoning

When within 15 blocks of the target location, the action bar turns gold: "You have reached the spot! Right-Click to summon him!"
• 40 ticks of Nautilus particles and Warden heartbeat sound.
• Giant 200-particle water eruption + Elder Guardian curse sound.
• Server-wide broadcast announcement!
• Pearl converts into permanent Angler's Tracker.

🌊
Open-Water Safety System: If the summoning location is in deep ocean or open water, CoreSuite automatically places a mystical Lily Pad directly beneath the NPC's feet and locks the water level, ensuring the Angler never drowns or glitches through the seabed!

The 5 Philosophical Prompts & Choice Architecture

How the Angler tests your character before unveiling your quest path.

Upon summoning, the Ancient Angler rolls 1 of 5 scenario prompts. He then randomly presents 3 distinct dialogue choices corresponding to 3 different paths. Players can click the chat options directly (which runs /fish quest <1|2|3>) or type 1, 2, or 3 in chat.

Scenario 1: The Surrendering Sailor
"*Cough*... I watched a man surrender to the water 'ere yesterday. Didn't thrash. Didn't scream. Just sank like a stone. Why d'ye think he stayed so quiet?"

Choices test whether you value survival instinct, greed, hunting monsters, silence, or foolish hubris.

Scenario 2: The Iron Hook of the Soul
"Look at 'em... Every fish bites the iron thinkin' it's a blessin'. Tell me, youngster, what's the iron hook that caught your soul?"

Explores your deepest desires: conquering currents, sunken riches, thrill of the catch, or stillness.

Scenario 3: The Broken Compass
"I once watched a ship's captain throw his compass into the sea. His crew wept, but he smiled. Why d'ye think he destroyed his only guide?"

Tests faith in intuition, surrender to destiny, defiance of the deep, or boundless arrogance.

Scenario 4: The Protective Devotion
"There's a fish that swallows its own young to protect them from the storm. Tell me, mortal — what would ye swallow to keep what matters safe?"

Examines sacrificial devotion, hunger for plunder, predatory rage, or quiet contemplation.

Scenario 5: The Mariana Trench
"The deepest trench holds no light, no warmth, no mercy. Yet something lives there still. What drives a creature to thrive where everything says it should perish?"

Tests pure endurance, bottomless greed, primordial hunger, or reckless bravado.

Offerings, Riddles & The Sacrifice Hint System

Turn-in interactions, riddle recitations, and paid hint mechanics.

Whenever you interact with the Angler during an active quest stage, you are given 3 interactive choices:

1

[✦ Offer the Catch]

Submits the required fish from your inventory or fish bag. If correct, advances you to the next stage or claims your relic!

2

[Listen to Riddle]

The Angler repeats his poetic riddle for free: "The deep speaks in riddles, kid. Only those who listen survive."

3

[✗ Sacrifice for Hint]

Pay $25,000 + 1x Rare/Epic/Legendary fish tribute to force the Angler to speak plain English (e.g. "A Char. Bring me a Char.").

⚠️
What if you choose Hint with NO rare fish tribute?
If you attempt to buy a hint without a qualifying fish in your inventory or fish bag, the Angler punishes you with a 3-way roulette penalty:
• Roll 1 (33%): Cursed with Slowness I for 3 minutes.
• Roll 2 (33%): Cursed with Mining Fatigue I for 3 minutes.
• Roll 3 (33%): Seizes $50,000 instead of $25,000 with lightning and thunder!
⚠️

The Consequences of Deceit & Failure

Exponential fines and blinding debuffs for empty-handed attempts.

The Ancient Angler does not tolerate mortals who attempt to trick him with empty hands or incorrect fish. Every time you click [Offer Catch] without the required items, your persistent Fail Counter increases:

Fail Streak Money Fine Deducted Debuff Effects Applied Angler Action & Sound
1st Fail $10,000 Blindness (8 seconds) Lightning strike sound + angry rebuke
2nd Fail $20,000 Blindness (11 seconds) Rebukes your deceit
3rd Fail $40,000 Blindness (14s) + Slowness (25s) Disorients player movement
4th Fail $80,000 Blindness (17s) + Slowness (25s) Furious storm winds
5th+ Fails $160,000 – $500,000 (Cap) Blindness (20-30s) + Slowness + Nausea (15s) Maximum abyssal curse

* Fines allow negative balance debt if configured. Always check your fish bag and inventory before clicking [Offer Catch]!

Complete Command Encyclopedia

Comprehensive reference of all player and administrator commands.

Player Commands

All players have access to the following fishing commands:

Command Arguments Description
/fish None Opens your primary Fishing Overview GUI showing stats, level, and quick links.
/fish bag None Opens your multi-page Fish Bag to store custom catches safely without filling inventory.
/fish buybag None Purchases additional Fish Bag pages (up to 10 pages total, requiring higher levels & cash).
/fish quest <1|2|3|cancel> Selects dialogue options or turn-in actions during an active Ancient Angler dialogue.
/fish log / /fish info None Opens your Fishing Logbook & Collection GUI showing every discovered fish species.
/fish sell None Opens the interactive Fish Merchant GUI to sell custom catches for cash.
/fish bait None Opens the Bait Shop GUI to purchase Earthworm, Gilded Spinner, Tide's Blessing, or Mythical Chum.
/fish fatigue None Checks your current fishing fatigue percentage, penalty tiers, and recovery status.
/fish pond None Broadcasts the coordinates and status of any currently forming or active Legendary Fish Pond.
/fish tournament None (alias: tourney) Opens the weekly Fishing Tournament leaderboard and prize pool overview.
/fish top None Opens the All-Time Leaderboards GUI for total catches, largest fish, and highest levels.
/fish hof None (alias: halloffame) Views the Hall of Fame honoring legendary tournament champions of past seasons.
/fish shop None Opens the General Fishing Supplies Shop GUI.

Administrator & Staff Commands

Requires permission coresuite.customfishing.admin:

Admin Command Permission Description & Example

/fish admin addxp <player> <amount> coresuite.customfishing.admin Adds fishing experience points directly to the player's profile.
/fish admin fatigue <player> <amount> coresuite.customfishing.admin Sets player fatigue score (0 to 100%). Useful for debugging fatigue penalties.

Database Architecture & Staff Troubleshooting

Database schema, asynchronous execution, and troubleshooting steps.

All quest progress is stored in the coresuite_fisherman_quest table in MySQL or SQLite, with thread-safe cached lookups and asynchronous persistence:

Column Name Data Type Default Description
uuid TEXT PRIMARY KEY - The player's unique Minecraft UUID string.
stage INTEGER 0 Current stage (0 = none, 10 = intro, 101-104 = Path 1, 201-203 = Path 2, etc.).
times_completed INTEGER 0 Lifetime completions of any Ancient Angler questline.
times_got_tc INTEGER 0 Times the player successfully obtained the Tireless Caster enchantment upgrade.
times_disrespected INTEGER 0 Number of times the player picked rude/insolent dialogue options.
fail_count INTEGER 0 Current consecutive fail streak (used to calculate exponential fines).
has_fossilized_anchor INTEGER 0 Flag (0 or 1) indicating if the player has ever received the Fossilized Anchor.
has_stormcaller_trident INTEGER 0 Flag (0 or 1) indicating if the player has ever received the Stormcaller's Trident.

Staff Troubleshooting Guide

1

Player Lost Their Pearl / Tracker

Run /fish admin legendary givemap <player>. This generates a brand new Tidecaller's Pearl pointing towards a valid target coordinate.

2

Player Stuck on a Stage

Check their stage via database or use /fish admin legendary setstage <player> <stage> to advance or restart their current step.

3

Complete Quest Reset

Run /fish admin legendary reset <player>. This sets stage to 0, despawns any active NPC, and clears dialogue cooldowns.
