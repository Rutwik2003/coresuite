# Server-wide event proposal: The Frozen Kingdom

This is a design proposal, not an implemented module or scheduled event. It supersedes the small weekly activity suggestions as the proposed large event format.

## Format

A seven-day adventure in a dedicated event world, open to established and brand-new players. Equal event kits, free entry and supplied consumables prevent existing wealth or gear from determining participation. Keep personal survival inventories outside the event and restore them safely on exit.

## Map and activities

- Frozen Village: introductory quest, event journal, rewards and social hub.
- Abandoned Mine: cooperative exploration and collection objectives.
- Icebound Ruins: puzzles, hidden rooms and discoveries.
- Frost Arena: short combat challenges with selectable difficulty.
- Mountain Pass: checkpoint-based parkour and obstacle courses.
- Royal Citadel: final cooperative encounter.

Exploration, puzzles, combat, parkour and community objectives all award progress. A player should not need PvP skill or expensive equipment to complete the main event.

## Week progression

Restore the village beacon, repair the bridge, unlock the archives and break the citadel seal. Community contributions advance these stages; scheduled fallback unlocks prevent low attendance from blocking the finale. Targets need calibration to actual activity, not an assumed large population.

Keep an individual journal with introduction, activity variety, discovery and contribution milestones. Add catch-up missions so late arrivals can still earn the main reward. Activities must work solo or with a small group even though the event is advertised server-wide.

## Rewards and limits

Use guaranteed milestone rewards, a completion cosmetic or collectible, selected UC/crate keys and decorative souvenirs. Keep leaderboard prizes optional. Cap repeatable token earnings and high-value redemptions. Do not sell tokens for survival money, award boss-shop equipment directly, or create unlimited cash/key farms. Final reward counts and commands require a separate agreed configuration.

## Finale and promotion

Run two announced citadel sessions with beacon defence, energy delivery, crystal objectives and a final assault. Scale for the participating group and include support roles. Leave a replay briefly available for players who miss the scheduled sessions.

Advertise the date, free entry, equal kits and guaranteed completion reward in one clear announcement. Publish a trailer or screenshots from the actual map, introduce new districts through the week, and post a recap afterwards. Do not promise content until the map and mechanics are tested.
