# CoreSuite Wiki

CoreSuite brings configurable gameplay and administration modules to a Paper server: world bosses, fishing, rewards, player orders, farming, archaeology, magic and server integrations.

**Documentation snapshot: 28 October 2026.** These pages describe the current working source and public preview. They do not promise that every feature is production-ready or compatible with every Minecraft version.

## Start here

1. [Install CoreSuite](installation.md) on a separate test server.
2. Learn [configuration and module controls](configuration.md).
3. Find your feature in the [module directory](modules/index.md).
4. Check [commands](reference/commands.md), [permissions](reference/permissions.md) and [dependencies](integrations.md).
5. Follow the [testing checklist](testing.md) before opening your server.

The public preview starts with **all modules disabled** on a fresh installation. The ordinary MeerMC build has different defaults. Existing installations retain their saved settings; changing the JAR does not reset them.

This wiki documents configurable systems, not fixed promises about prices, loot or boss difficulty. Your installed configuration is the authority for your server's values.
